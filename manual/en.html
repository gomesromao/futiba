START PLAYING

KICK-OFF.

FUTIBA is videogame football the old way: quick passes, slide tackles that hurt, goalkeepers who decide. Fewer buttons, more reading of the game: the same button does different things with and without the ball.

1

PICK A MODE

Friendly to go straight to a match, Championship, Cup and Career Mode for a campaign, Indoor for futsal, wall mode, beach and street.

2

PICK A TEAM

Over 150 teams, each with a squad, ratings, formation and a portrait for every player.

3

SET UP THE TEAM

Adjust the line-up and the formation. In futsal, wall mode and beach, pick who plays outfield.

4

PLAY

Read the pitch, move the ball and choose the moment to shoot.

THE MAIN MENU

  • FRIENDLY: a one-off match, with the stadium, time of day and weather you want. Two players can play.
  • CHAMPIONSHIP: 16 clubs, 15 rounds, round robin.
  • CUP: FUTIBA CUP (knockout of 8 or 16), CHAMPIONS FUTIBA and WORLD CUP.
  • EXTRAS: PENALTIES, TRAINING and SCENARIOS.
  • INDOOR: futsal, wall mode, BRAZUCA CUP, FUTSAL CHALLENGE, BEACH MODE, BEACH CHALLENGE and STREET.
  • CHALLENGE: you against a super team. Beat them and they play for you.
  • CAREER MODE: the MASTER LEAGUE.
  • SUPER WORLD CUP: shows up when you become world champion in the Master League.
  • EDITOR: import mod, team and stadiums, and CUSTOMIZE.
  • ACHIEVEMENTS: 12 achievements and the sticker album.
  • OPTIONS: language, volume, frame, filter, half length, difficulty and SPEECH.

BEFORE THE WHISTLE

FROM THE MENU TO THE CENTRE CIRCLE.

The path to a match on the pitch. On every screen, the game shows the buttons for that moment at the bottom.

1 · TEAMS

←/→ change club. Confirm the home team, then the away team. ↓ goes to INFO, with the club's story.

2 · PRE-MATCH

Choose who you control (home, away or just watch), the stadium (18), the time of day (day, sunset, night) and the weather (sun or rain).

3 · LINE-UP

Confirm one player and then another to swap them, bench included. At the bottom: formation, AUTO (the game picks), OPPONENT (see the rival) and CONFIRM.

4 · OPENING

The camera tours the stand and comes down to the pitch. START skips it.

5 · COIN TOSS

←/→ pick heads or tails; confirm flips the coin.

6 · SIDE

If you won: ↑ keeps the ball; ← or → picks the end. If the rival won, you watch their choice.

IN THE MATCH

WHAT IS ON SCREEN.

  • SCORE: top left, with the club codes.
  • CLOCK: top right.
  • RADAR: bottom centre: dots for both teams and the ball.
  • NAMES: bottom: your player on the right, the nearest rival on the left. Yellow chip = card; cross = injury.
  • BALLOONS: players talk: they call for the ball, complain, celebrate.
PAUSE

RESUME, TACTICS and QUIT MATCH.

TACTICS

Formation and up to 3 substitutions. They apply at the next restart.

HALF-TIME

Line-up screen to change the team. In the 2nd half the ends swap.

GOAL

The scorer celebrates, the rival laments, then comes the replay. START shortens it.

REFEREE

Fouls, yellow, red and injuries. A red on your team: back to the line-up to reorganise.

FULL TIME

Summary with the goals. REMATCH, NEW TEAMS or MODE SELECT.

GAME MODES

PICK YOUR FIGHT.

IN AND PLAY

FRIENDLY

  • Two teams, pre-match, line-up and kick-off.
  • Match length in OPTIONS: 3, 5 or 7 real minutes per half.
  • No campaign: the score ends there. The rivalry doesn't.
THE WHOLE SEASON COUNTS

CHAMPIONSHIP

  • 16 clubs, single round robin, 15 rounds. Win 3, draw 1.
  • Tie-break: points, goal difference, goals scored.
  • A red card or injury on your team rules the player out of the next round.
  • Every club has its own save.
LOSE AND YOU'RE OUT

CUP

  • CUP > FUTIBA CUP. Bracket of 8 or 16, single match.
  • A draw? It goes to penalties, played by you.
  • Top scorers, cards and injuries follow the run.
ONE KICK. ONE DIVE.

PENALTIES

  • It lives in EXTRAS. Set the order of the 10 takers for each team.
  • 5 kicks each and then sudden death.
  • Taking: arrows aim (left, centre, right; high, middle, low) and the pass button shoots.
  • In goal: arrows pick the corner and the pass button dives.

INDOOR

FUTSAL AND WALL MODE.

TEAM SET-UP (both modes)

  • Mark who plays outfield with the pass button.
  • The sweeper (GL) is the substitute who can replace the goalkeeper to attack: pick him with the shoot button. On touch, the game suggests the sweeper and B goes back.
  • ←/→ decide whether the match starts with the sweeper on.
  • START moves on to the rival team and then starts the match.
  • SELECT swaps the flying keeper without pausing: TAB on the keyboard, BACK on the controller.
4 OUTFIELD + 1 IN GOAL

FUTSAL

  • Two halves of 3 real minutes (the clock shows 30 minutes each). The half-length option does not apply.
  • Kick-ins taken with the foot.
  • Fouls add up. From the team's 6th on, every foul is a direct free kick from the second mark, with no wall (inside the box, a penalty). They reset at half-time.
  • The goalkeeper may cross the halfway line.
  • Free substitutions, with the ball in play.
  • A sent-off player does not return. A team down to goalkeeper + 1 loses by forfeit.
3 OUTFIELD + 1 IN GOAL

WALL MODE

  • First to 5 goals wins.
  • The ball bounces off the wall: no throw-ins, corners or goal kicks.
  • No referee, no fouls, no cards.
  • The goalkeeper may not cross the halfway line: if he does, the ball goes to the rival. The sweeper may.
  • Free outfield substitutions, with the ball in play (pause → TACTICS / SUBS).

NEW MODES

CHALLENGE AND COPA BRAZUCA.

YOU AGAINST A SUPER TEAM

CHALLENGE

  • Main menu > CHALLENGE. A cutscene introduces the rival; START skips it, confirm speeds it up.
  • The super team plays at home. You pick only your team and play away at the SPECIAL stadium.
  • The CPU plays at the top level, whatever the difficulty.
  • Lost? Rematch. Won? TEAM UNLOCKED: the super team plays for you in every mode.
  • Beat the first super team to unlock the second.
KNOCKOUT ON THE COURT

COPA BRAZUCA

  • Indoor > COPA BRAZUCA. Futsal knockout of 8, drawn from 12 court teams.
  • Before each match you set up only your team, sweeper included.
  • A draw goes to penalties on the court itself, one kick at a time.
  • Every title unlocks the champion for every mode.

PITCH TIPS

  • Chip and lob: hold the sprint button and shoot. A quick tap chips from close; held, the ball rises and drops fast. Aim it and dose it.
  • Goalkeeper rushing out: one on one, he comes for the ball. Dribble, chip or pass first.
  • Shot from halfway: almost everyone hits it weak and bouncing. Only the cannon-footed stars fire the low rocket.
  • Cutscenes: Cup and Championship open with their own, and getting knocked out has one too.

CUPS AND CAREER

TROPHIES, NEWSPAPERS AND A CAREER.

32 NATIONAL TEAMS

WORLD CUP

  • CUP > WORLD CUP. Pick your team first: there are 40, just for this mode, each with its own coach and squad.
  • FIXED DRAW: the classic groups. If your team is not among the 32, it takes Australia's place. RANDOM DRAW: 32 of the 40, with the 8 strongest seeded.
  • 8 groups, round of 16, quarters, semis, third place match and final.
  • Before and after every match the newspaper comes out: ←/→ turn the page, confirm moves on and back closes it.
  • Stadiums get World Cup boards. CONTINUE CUP picks the run back up.
16 TEAM KNOCKOUT

CHAMPIONS FUTIBA

  • CUP > CHAMPIONS FUTIBA.
  • Europe's giants: 10 fixed + 6 drawn.
  • 16 team knockout to the final.
CAREER MODE

MASTER LEAGUE

  • CAREER MODE > NEW CAREER or LOAD.
  • 4 divisions of 12 clubs, Serie A to Serie D.
  • In the club menu: play or simulate, friendly, calendar, tables, cups, squad, market, sponsorship, newspaper, trophies, coach, export team and save.
  • EXPORT TEAM keeps your team in up to 5 slots (ML1 to ML5).
  • Become world champion and the SUPER WORLD CUP shows up in the main menu: a 16 team knockout.

STREET AND BEACH

NO GRASS.

2 AGAINST 2

STREET

  • INDOOR > STREET. Upper street against lower street, 3 minutes.
  • Flip flop goals: a goal only counts from inside the box.
  • The place is drawn: favela, downtown, countryside, beach, night, Amazon, school, rooftop or village.
  • Without the ball the buttons change: pass switches player, shoot tackles and cross is a slide.
  • Two players can play. There are no shirt numbers in the street.
BEACH SOCCER

BEACH MODE

  • INDOOR > BEACH MODE. 11 beach only teams.
  • 3 periods of 12 minutes (2 real minutes each). A draw stands: no extra time and no penalties.
  • Bare feet, kick-ins and free kicks with no wall.
  • Little mounds in the sand knock the ball off course.
  • BEACH CHALLENGE: beat the sand national team and it plays for you in the other modes (except the Brazuca Cup).

FUTSAL CHALLENGE (Indoor): the rival waits on the orange court. Beat them and they play for you.

2 PLAYERS, EXTRAS AND EDITOR

THERE'S MORE.

2 PLAYERS

  • In FRIENDLY, FUTSAL, WALL MODE, BEACH MODE and STREET, pick 1 PLAYER or 2 PLAYERS.
  • Each player presses a button on their own device to join (keyboard or controller). 1P is home and 2P is away.
  • With fewer than two devices, 2 PLAYERS is greyed out.

EXTRAS

  • PENALTIES: straight to the spot.
  • TRAINING: short lessons in 5 categories (BASICS, ADVANCED, DEFENDING, AERIAL BALL, SET PIECES). Each step shows the button of the device you are using. There is also FREE MODE, starters vs reserves.
  • SCENARIOS: 12 historic matches. You come in for the second half, with the score and the minute of the day, and a mission. Lost? Try again.

EDITOR

  • IMPORT MOD: mods made in the site's MOD STUDIO (futiba.vercel.app/mod). On PC, the mod folder sits next to the game.
  • IMPORT TEAM: a single team, exported from CUSTOMIZE. On PC, the times folder sits next to the game.
  • IMPORT STADIUMS: up to 5 stadiums made in the MOD STUDIO. On PC, the estadios folder sits next to the game. The stands in these stadiums don't move.
  • CUSTOMIZE: edit teams and players.

IN THE MATCH

  • Offside: on the pitch, the referee calls it and the screen shows OFFSIDE.
  • Shirt numbers in every mode except the street.
  • SPEECH (in OPTIONS): turns the coach and player speech bubbles on or off.
  • Idle players don't stand like statues: each one has its own way of waiting.

ANDROID · TOUCH

ON-SCREEN CONTROLS.

The game sits in the middle, in 4:3. The controls sit on the black bars at the sides, without covering the pitch.

THE GOLDEN BUTTON

When a screen is waiting for you, the right button gets a blinking golden ring. In menus it is A (with the word OK). In the futsal and wall set-up it is START. In the match: START at the opening and the replay; A at the coin toss, the pause and full time.

TIPS

  • You can press several buttons at once: d-pad + L1 + shoot, for example.
  • To cross while sprinting, release L1 first: with L1 held, C becomes the rainbow flick.
  • In futsal, with L1 held, the pass always goes in behind.
  • Penalty in a match: d-pad picks the side, A shoots low, B shoots high.

ANDROID · CONTROLLER · OPTIONS

WITH A CONTROLLER.

The Android controller version is for handhelds with physical buttons (R36S, Anbernic, Retroid…) or a phone with a paired controller. Nothing shows on screen: the game sits in 4:3 in the middle and the buttons are the same as the controller on PC (page 12).

OPTIONS

  • LANGUAGE: Portuguese, English or Spanish.
  • VOLUME: master, music and effects.
  • FRAME: PC only: TV, album, poster, comic or FUTIBA art on the sides.
  • FILTER: NORMAL or TUBE TV.
  • HALF LENGTH: 3, 5 or 7 real minutes per half (pitch).
  • DIFFICULTY: easy, normal or hard (changes the CPU). In cups, from the semi-final on the CPU goes up a level.
  • SPEECH: turns the coach and player speech bubbles on or off.

SECRETS

Win the Cup or the Championship and two hidden teams show up. Beat the CHALLENGE and the super team plays for you. Every COPA BRAZUCA title unlocks the champion. And anyone who played videogames in the 90s knows: the opening screen always has a code.

ACHIEVEMENTS

12 achievements unlock stickers in the FUTIBA album: first win, comeback, thrashing, hat-trick, own goal, playing a man down…
